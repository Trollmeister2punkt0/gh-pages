Projects

During my time at The Game Assembly I have been a part of 5 finished game projects. One is under development and set to be finished April 11. 

During all these projects I have been a part of a team of students working together to create something original. We as a group needed to work together and create the best possible game under the circumstances we had. I have been working with different teams of people for all of these projects.
