I am 27 years old and interested in all things technical. Currently I am a student at The Game Assembly, studying game programming. I love video games and have for as long as I can remember.

During my first year at The Game Assembly I was fairly new to coding and wanted to be able to solve my coding challenges in any way possible. During my time at the school I truly learned that creating something for the end user is much more rewarding than creating something for myself. To that cause I have during my second year spent a lot of time creating and modifying my tools and utilities together with other students to create a better experience.

I'm a social person and I feel communication is a major component for success in any situation. I have an active lifestyle and I play tennis a few times a week. 

Driven by my interest in all things technical, I am currently building my own car.
